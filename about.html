Who We Are

Built for homeowners, not developers.

Most real estate advice is built for investors. Built Capital Advisory exists for the person who already owns the property and just wants to know their real options.

Our Reason for Being

Homeowners deserve the same analysis developers get.

Developers and investors have teams that run the numbers on every property before they touch it — zoning potential, subdivision value, renovation ROI. Homeowners almost never get that same look at their own property.

Built Capital Advisory was founded to close that gap: bringing developer-grade analysis directly to the people who already own the lot, so they can make the most of it — whether that means selling, building, or holding.

What We Believe

No pressure

We don't make money by pushing you toward a sale. Our job is to show you the real numbers — what you do with them is entirely yours to decide.

Plain language

Zoning codes and appraisal math translated into what it actually means for your bottom line — no jargon, no fine print you need a lawyer to read.

Every option on the table

Sell, subdivide, renovate, rebuild, or hold — we look at all of them, not just the one that's easiest to close.
